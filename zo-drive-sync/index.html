Zo Drive Sync

A personal tool for synchronizing study materials and lecture-recording outputs between the owner’s Zo Computer and Google Drive.

What it does

With the owner’s Google authorization, the tool lists and downloads selected source files, uploads transcripts, subtitles, reviewed recordings and reports to designated Drive folders, and checks file metadata and checksums to verify transfers.

The related recording workflow uses Google AI Studio for transcription and audio review, followed by local Whisper comparison and speech alignment on Zo. AI-generated text and timing may contain errors.

This application is for the owner’s personal use. This public website contains application information only; it does not provide access to recordings, study files or the private application.
