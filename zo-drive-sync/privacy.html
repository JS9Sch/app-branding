Privacy policy

Zo Drive Sync · Updated 2026-10-20

Purpose and data accessed

Zo Drive Sync is a personal application operated by its account owner. It accesses authorized study files and lecture recordings, generated transcripts and subtitles, review reports, and metadata such as file names, IDs, folder paths, sizes, modification times and checksums. These are used to locate selected materials, process recordings, synchronize outputs and verify transfers.

Google authorization

The owner grants Google Drive access through OAuth. The synchronization workflow uses that access to list, read, download, create and upload files in the configured workflow. The scope shown on the Google consent screen may grant wider access than the folders selected in the tool’s configuration. OAuth tokens are retained in the owner’s private runtime configuration to support unattended transfers; they are not published on this website or in its repository.

Processing and service providers

Files and processing records are stored on the owner’s Zo Computer and in designated Google Drive folders. Selected recordings are provided to Google AI Studio for transcription and per-cue audio review. Whisper comparison and speech alignment run locally on the Zo server. Zo’s configured AI service may process task context and transcript excerpts when orchestrating or reviewing this workflow. Google, Zo and any configured AI provider process data under their applicable terms and settings; this policy does not promise that third-party services never retain data or use it to improve their services.

The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. The workflow does not automatically make Drive recordings public or widen sharing permissions. Any access needed by a separate AI Studio account must be granted by the owner.

Retention and deletion

Source recordings, prior versions, generated outputs and review evidence are retained for study and auditing until the owner removes them or changes the configured workflow. This tool does not apply the camera archive application’s seven-day deletion policy. Deleting a Drive copy does not automatically delete local copies or previously submitted AI Studio content.

Your control

The owner can stop the synchronization or recording service, change the selected folders, delete retained files, and revoke OAuth access in Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized API access but does not itself delete existing copies or outputs. Data submitted to third-party services must also be managed using those services’ controls.

This website and contact

This information website is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no application login, analytics scripts or access to private Drive files. GitHub may process ordinary web-request information when serving it. Contact the operator through the support email displayed on the application’s Google OAuth consent screen.
